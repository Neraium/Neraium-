Initial focus

Review questions, not diagnoses.

These examples describe the kinds of system behavior an evaluation may help a team review. They do not assign root cause.

01

Central plants and chilled-water systems

Chillers, pumps, towers, heat exchangers, valves, and distribution loops.

Review questions
Has delivered cooling response changed relative to plant power under comparable load and staging?

Does differential pressure respond differently to flow across similar modes?
02

Pumping and water systems

Lift, transfer, distribution, domestic, and industrial water infrastructure.

Review questions
Has flow or pressure response changed relative to speed, demand, or valve position?

Has the relationship between demand, level, flow, and replenishment changed persistently?
03

Resort and large-facility infrastructure

Mechanical systems operating across schedules, seasons, occupancy, and facility demand.

Review questions
Does a subsystem respond differently under comparable schedule, load, occupancy, or seasonal context?

Is a change sustained beyond normal operating transitions?
04

Other bounded interconnected systems

Equipment networks with repeated operating states and measurable response.

Review questions
Can the available history support a reliable operating reference?

Is a relationship change sufficiently supported to warrant engineering review?

System suitability

Fit is established, not assumed.

A Neraium Historical Evaluation considers one system and one review question against the history actually available. Some systems will not have the coverage or context needed for a responsible conclusion.

  • Interconnected equipmentBehavior depends on more than one isolated asset.
  • Measurable relationshipsSignals describe command, demand, energy, flow, pressure, temperature, or output.
  • Repeated operating statesComparable modes occur often enough to establish behavior.
  • Usable historical telemetryApproved history covers the question with usable timestamps and signals.
