System suitability

What makes a system a good fit?

Start with one system that has enough history to establish a baseline and compare it with a separate evaluation dataset.

  • Interconnected equipmentBehavior depends on more than one isolated asset.
  • Measurable relationshipsSignals describe command, demand, energy, flow, pressure, temperature, or output.
  • Repeated operating statesComparable modes occur often enough to establish behavior.
  • Usable historical telemetryThe baseline and evaluation datasets have usable timestamps and signals.
