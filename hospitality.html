Facility continuity

Facilities directors can prioritize developing central-plant and circulation concerns before they become urgent investigations. Engineers can review comparable operating periods to help plan field checks around occupancy, guest services, and park schedules.

Utility and maintenance costs

Compare cooling output or delivered flow with energy and pumping effort. Supporting evidence helps maintenance teams assess whether inspection, cleaning, or further analysis is warranted, potentially avoiding unnecessary work and operating expense.

These examples describe intended applicability, not verified customer deployments. A finding supports investigation; it does not establish root cause or failure timing.
