Why Neraium exists

The harder question begins after the dashboard.

Teams already have alarms, trends, controls, work orders, and deep site knowledge. The harder question is whether the relationships that define system behavior have persistently changed even while individual values appear acceptable.

Neraium exists to make that question reviewable with evidence and limitations intact.
