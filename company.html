Company

Built from the gap between what the dashboard shows and what the system is actually doing.

Neraium helps facility teams turn changing system behavior into a disciplined engineering review—without displacing the controls, alarms, or people responsible for operations.

Why Neraium exists

Operations already has signals. The missing layer is evidence.

Facilities have alarms, controls, trends, work orders, and experienced operators. Yet a system can begin behaving differently before any one signal explains why—or before it crosses an alarm threshold.

Neraium was started to make that operational gap reviewable: preserve the relationships, context, and limits in the telemetry, then give the engineering team a clearer place to begin field investigation.

Origin

Practical systems work shaped the question.

Founder Craig Curtis has worked around facility operations, mechanical systems, water systems, and field-level infrastructure. That experience made the recurring problem concrete: operators can have substantial information and sound judgment, while still lacking a disciplined way to show that the system’s relationships have changed.

Neraium is built to support operator and engineering judgment, not replace it.
