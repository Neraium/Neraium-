Company

Infrastructure intelligence built around evidence and restraint.

Neraium exists to help engineering and operations teams understand whether the behavior of interconnected physical infrastructure has persistently changed, without displacing the systems or people responsible for operating it.

Why Neraium exists

The harder question begins after the dashboard.

Operational teams already have alarms, trends, controls, work orders, and deep site knowledge. Individual values may still look acceptable while the relationships that define system response change over time.

Neraium is being built to establish expected operating behavior, surface evidence of persistent systemic change, and keep uncertainty and limitations visible for responsible review.

Operating principles

Technical credibility depends on clear boundaries.

01

Establish before judging

Qualify signals, context, and baseline behavior before drawing a later comparison.

02

Preserve uncertainty

Keep evidence, provenance, limitations, and plausible alternatives attached to every finding.

03

Protect authority

Remain read-only, outside the control path, and subordinate to engineering and operations judgment.

04

Allow insufficient evidence

Do not force a finding when the available telemetry cannot support a responsible conclusion.
