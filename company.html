Company

Built for practical infrastructure investigation.

Neraium is an infrastructure intelligence company focused on helping owners and technical teams turn approved operational telemetry into evidence they can review, challenge, and use responsibly.

Why Neraium exists

Operational teams already have dashboards, alarms, trends, controls, work orders, and deep site knowledge. The harder question is whether relationships across the system have changed persistently, what evidence supports that conclusion, and what remains unknown.

Operating principles

  • Stay read-only and outside the control path.
  • Preserve operating context, uncertainty, and limitations.
  • Keep engineering, operations, and maintenance judgment authoritative.
  • Minimize data requested for scoping and evaluation.
  • Treat no finding and insufficient evidence as valid outcomes.
