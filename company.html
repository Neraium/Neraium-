About Neraium

Built around an operational question.

How can teams understand a changing system when individual readings still look acceptable?

Our purpose

Make interconnected infrastructure easier to understand.

Neraium builds Systemic Infrastructure Intelligence for the people responsible for essential operations.

We focus on relationships between operational signals, persistent changes in system behavior, and evidence engineers can inspect. Human judgment remains authoritative.

Neraium is currently pre-revenue and developing its MVP. Our initial commercial markets are municipal water and wastewater utilities, and hospitality resorts and large integrated resort operations.
