Platform

Read-only infrastructure intelligence for relationship-level review.

Neraium analyzes persistent behavioral changes across interconnected operational systems, then preserves the finding, context, evidence, uncertainty, and limitations for human review.

Approved telemetry inputs

Exports or approved read-only sources can come from BAS, SCADA, historians, PLC-derived telemetry, databases, APIs, or secure files.

Baseline and comparison workflow

Signals are mapped to systems, modes, and operating context before baseline behavior is compared with a later period.

Persistence and relationships

Neraium looks for sustained relationship changes across comparable conditions rather than isolated threshold events.

Evidence Package production

When support is adequate, the analysis produces a review artifact with evidence, context, uncertainty, limitations, and a practical starting point.

Engineering and maintenance views

Engineering receives relationship evidence and context. Maintenance receives a concise view of what changed and what to check first.

Insufficient evidence

No finding and insufficient evidence are valid results when telemetry does not support a behavioral conclusion.

Where it fits

Beside the systems and people already operating the facility.

Neraium does not displace the operating stack. BAS, SCADA, historians, and PLCs continue their existing work; alarms and CMMS remain operational systems of record.

Engineers remain responsible for interpretation.

Operators remain responsible for operations.

Maintenance teams decide what field checks are justified.

Read-only boundary

The platform sits outside the control path. It never changes setpoints, issues commands, overrides equipment, or replaces engineering, operations, or maintenance judgment.

Historical exports can begin an evaluation.

Live source access, when used, must be approved and read-only.

Deployment and security controls are confirmed for each customer environment.
