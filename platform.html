Product capabilities

System behavior, not isolated anomaly scores.

Baseline learning

Build a usable operating reference.

Qualify suitable history and establish expected behavior where signal quality and context support it.

Relationship intelligence

Learn how the system responds together.

Evaluate command, load, flow, pressure, temperature, power, and delivered output as connected behavior.

Operating context

Preserve the conditions around behavior.

Retain mode, load, schedule, environment, equipment state, and other approved context.

Persistent-change analysis

Separate durable change from transient behavior.

Compare later behavior across relevant time scales and comparable operating conditions.

System-level findings

Turn change into a bounded review question.

Show engineers which relationships changed and where supported investigation can begin.

Evidence and provenance

Keep support and limits together.

Preserve comparison periods, supporting signals, uncertainty, provenance, limitations, and review state.

Continued analysis

Keep comparing against established behavior.

Once a defensible operating reference exists, later approved telemetry can be compared over time. Persistent departures become bounded questions for engineering review rather than unexplained alerts.

Operating boundary

Complements the operating stack.
