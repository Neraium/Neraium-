Privacy

Public inquiry, clearly bounded.

Neraium’s public website is for fit and scoping conversations. It is not a channel for operational or sensitive infrastructure information.

What the inquiry requests

Business context only.

The inquiry asks for name, work email, organization, facility or system type, a plain-language review question, and optional role, contact preference, and general data-availability context.

Do not include operational data, credentials, telemetry exports, facility diagrams, network information, signal details, or sensitive system information.

How the website handles it

An email draft, not a web upload.

The current public form does not upload details to Neraium or a form processor. It prepares a draft in the visitor’s email application so the visitor can review and choose whether to send it.

The public site does not load analytics or advertising scripts. Customer-approved channels for any later data exchange are coordinated separately.

  • No credentials requested
  • No operational telemetry requested
  • No security or network diagrams requested
  • No public-file upload
