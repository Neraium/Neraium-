Privacy

Privacy and public-inquiry boundaries.

Neraium’s public website is for scoping conversations. Do not include telemetry, credentials, security diagrams, or confidential operational details in the inquiry.

What the inquiry requests

The inquiry asks for business contact details, organization, facility or system type, a plain-language review question, and optional role, contact preference, and data-availability context.

How the website handles it

The current public form does not upload those details to Neraium or a form processor. It prepares a draft in the visitor’s email application so the visitor can review and choose whether to send it.

No credentials requested.

No operational telemetry requested.

No security diagrams requested.

The public site does not load analytics or advertising scripts.

Customer-approved channels are coordinated separately for data exchange.
