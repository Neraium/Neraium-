Privacy

The public site is for fit and scoping conversations, not operational or sensitive infrastructure information.

What the inquiry requests

The form asks for name, work email, organization, system or facility type, a plain-language review question, and optional role and contact preference.

Do not include telemetry, credentials, diagrams, network details, files, signal names, or other sensitive system information.

How the website handles it

When online submission is unavailable, you can prepare a draft from your entered details and open it in your email application. You choose whether to send it through your email provider. Nothing is uploaded through that draft path.

When configured, online submission uses Cloudflare for security verification and Zoho Mail for email delivery to Neraium. Contact details and your message are sent only when you submit. The website does not store submissions in a database or log their contents. Craig owns deletion of noncustomer inquiries from the Neraium mailbox within 90 days of the last correspondence, including associated received messages, Sent copies and Trash. Customer records follow a separately agreed retention policy. Forwarded or additional inquiry copies must have an assigned deletion owner under the same policy before online intake is enabled. Provider backups and service records follow the providers’ terms; immediate deletion from provider backups is not promised. Online intake remains unavailable until delivery, security verification and copy-retention arrangements are complete.

The public site does not load analytics or advertising scripts. To ask about access or deletion of an inquiry, email craig@neraium.com. Operational-data exchange is coordinated separately.

  • No credentials requested
  • No operational telemetry requested
  • No network or security diagrams requested
  • No public-file upload
