Privacy

The public site is for fit and scoping conversations, not operational or sensitive infrastructure information.

What the inquiry requests

The form asks for name, work email, organization, system or facility type, a plain-language review question, and optional role and contact preference.

Do not include telemetry, credentials, diagrams, network details, files, signal names, or other sensitive system information.

How the website handles it

When online submission is unavailable, you can prepare a draft from your entered details and open it in your email application. You choose whether to send it through your email provider. Nothing is uploaded through that draft path.

When configured, online submission uses Cloudflare for security verification and Zoho Mail for email delivery to Neraium. Contact details and your message are sent only when you submit. The website does not store submissions in a database or log their contents. Craig is responsible for deleting noncustomer inquiries from the Neraium Zoho mailbox within 90 days of the last correspondence, including Received, Sent and Trash copies. Customer records follow a separately agreed retention policy. Other mailbox rules, organization copies, archives, client exports and provider backups may create additional copies; their configuration and retention periods have not been inventoried and may follow separate settings or provider terms. The 90-day commitment applies to the stated mailbox copies and does not promise immediate removal from provider recovery or backup systems.

The public site does not load analytics or advertising scripts. To ask about access or deletion of an inquiry, email craig@neraium.com. Operational-data exchange is coordinated separately.
