Privacy

The public site is for fit and scoping conversations, not operational or sensitive infrastructure information.

What the inquiry requests

The form asks for name, work email, organization, system or facility type, a plain-language review question, and optional role and contact preference.

Do not include telemetry, credentials, diagrams, network details, files, signal names, or other sensitive system information.

How the website handles it

When online submission is unavailable, the form prepares an email draft in your email application. You choose whether to send it through your email provider. Nothing is uploaded through that draft path.

When configured, online submission uses Cloudflare for security verification and email delivery to Neraium. Contact details and your message are sent only when you submit. The website does not store submissions in a database or log their contents. Email and service records remain subject to the configured providers’ handling and Neraium’s approved inquiry-retention policy. The online form stays disabled until that policy is established.

The public site does not load analytics or advertising scripts. To ask about access or deletion of an inquiry, email craig@neraium.com. Operational-data exchange is coordinated separately.

  • No credentials requested
  • No operational telemetry requested
  • No network or security diagrams requested
  • No public-file upload
