Applications

Operational systems where relationships carry the signal.

Neraium is most useful when multiple related telemetry streams describe how a system responds under changing load, mode, schedule, or demand.

Resorts and casinos

Central plants, chilled water, cooling towers, domestic water, pumps, valves, pools, aquatic systems, guest-comfort infrastructure, and utilities without gaming or guest data.

Commercial buildings and campuses

Building and plant telemetry can show whether response patterns remain stable across operating modes, occupancy patterns, weather, and schedules.

Chilled-water and central utility plants

Power, flow, pressure, valve position, tower staging, temperature, and load relationships are practical candidates for historical comparison.

Water and pumping infrastructure

Lift, transfer, distribution, and industrial water systems benefit from evidence that separates delivered output from command or power demand.

Industrial facilities

Approved historian, database, API, PLC-derived, or secure-export telemetry may support focused system investigations.

Expansion environments

Cruise and maritime systems, hospitals, universities, airports, and data centers are candidates when telemetry and review ownership are available.

Good starting systems

A useful candidate has related measurements, enough history to establish a baseline, known modes or context, and an engineering owner who can review the evidence.
