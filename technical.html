System suitability

Applicability is defined by system characteristics.

A system is a stronger evaluation candidate when the operating question can be represented by historical telemetry and meaningful relationships across equipment and context.

  • Interconnected equipmentBehavior depends on more than one isolated asset.
  • Measurable signal relationshipsCommand, demand, energy, flow, pressure, temperature, or output can be compared.
  • Repeated operating statesComparable modes or conditions occur often enough to establish a reference.
  • Historical telemetryApproved history covers the system question with usable timestamps and signals.
  • Meaningful operating contextLoad, mode, schedule, environment, or equipment state can be preserved.
  • Persistent behavioral patternsThe question concerns durable system behavior, not a one-time event alone.

Application boundary

Not a claim of universal coverage.

Neraium should not be assumed to support every facility, industrial process, control platform, or data source. Fit is established around one bounded system, one review question, and the telemetry actually available.

No supported-integration list is implied.

No deployment or customer claim is implied.

No finding is guaranteed.

Historical telemetry may be insufficient for the question.
