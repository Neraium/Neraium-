System suitability

Fit is established, not assumed.

Neraium evaluates one bounded system and one review question against the telemetry actually available. Some systems are strong candidates; others may lack the signal quality, context, or repeated behavior needed for a responsible evaluation.

  • Interconnected equipmentBehavior depends on more than one isolated asset.
  • Measurable relationshipsSignals describe command, demand, energy, flow, pressure, temperature, or output.
  • Repeated operating statesComparable modes occur often enough to establish behavior.
  • Usable historical telemetryApproved history covers the question with usable timestamps and signals.
  • Relevant operating contextLoad, mode, schedule, environment, or equipment state can be preserved.
  • A persistent-behavior questionThe question concerns durable system behavior rather than a one-time event.
