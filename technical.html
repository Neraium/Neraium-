Where relationship monitoring earns its place.

Neraium is intended for complex operational systems where multiple signals, known operating context, and engineering review can make persistent relationship change useful.

Fit criteria

A system first, not a sector first.

Suitability is established from the data and the operating question.

01 / Signals

Related telemetry exists

Pressure, flow, temperature, demand, commands, states, or other signals describe connected system behavior.

02 / History

A baseline can be established

Approved historical data covers enough normal operation to represent relevant modes and ranges.

03 / Context

Operating changes can be explained

Schedules, staging, maintenance, known interventions, and demand conditions are available for review.

04 / Question

The team has a review goal

Engineers can state which system behavior needs better visibility and how a useful finding would be judged.

Poor-fit conditions

When Neraium may not be useful.

  • Only one isolated signal is available
  • Historical data is too sparse to establish behavior
  • Operating modes cannot be distinguished
  • Signal meaning or timing cannot be validated
  • No engineering owner is available to review findings
  • The use case requires automated equipment control
