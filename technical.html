Applications

Operational systems where relationships carry the signal.

Neraium is most useful when multiple related telemetry streams describe how a system responds under changing load, mode, schedule, or demand.

Resorts and casinos

Operational system: Central plants, domestic water, pumps, valves, aquatic systems, comfort infrastructure, and utilities without gaming or guest data.

Example telemetry relationships: Load, flow, pressure, temperature, speed, tower staging, schedule, and weather relationships.

Investigation value: Separates persistent infrastructure behavior changes from transient noise across high-occupancy operating modes.

Commercial buildings and campuses

Operational system: Building automation and utility systems serving multiple schedules, loads, and seasons.

Example telemetry relationships: Air and water temperature response, valve command response, pump differential pressure, and occupancy schedules.

Investigation value: Helps facilities teams decide which system trend deserves engineering review before changing controls.

Chilled-water and utility plants

Operational system: Chillers, pumps, towers, valves, heat exchangers, distribution loops, and utility metering.

Example telemetry relationships: Power to delivered flow, condenser-water response, staging sequence, pressure, flow, and temperature relationships.

Investigation value: Packages plant behavior changes with context so teams can target inspection and instrumentation checks.

Water and pumping infrastructure

Operational system: Lift, transfer, distribution, industrial water, and pumping systems.

Example telemetry relationships: Pump status, speed, flow, pressure, level, valve command, and delivered output relationships.

Investigation value: Supports investigation of persistent changes in delivered output under comparable demand.

Industrial facilities

Operational system: Historian, PLC-derived, database, API, or file-export telemetry from process-adjacent infrastructure.

Example telemetry relationships: Utility demand, equipment command, temperature, pressure, flow, and production-context relationships when approved.

Investigation value: Helps operational teams review infrastructure behavior without giving Neraium control authority.

Cruise and maritime

Operational system: Shipboard hotel services, chilled water, pumps, HVAC support systems, and utility equipment.

Example telemetry relationships: Mode, voyage segment, load, ambient condition, flow, pressure, and temperature relationships.

Investigation value: Supports bounded review where connectivity and operational windows are constrained.

Hospitals, universities, airports, and data centers

Operational system: Mission-critical campuses with telemetry-rich plants, distribution systems, and redundancy.

Example telemetry relationships: Redundant sensor agreement, load response, staging, temperature, flow, and pressure relationships.

Investigation value: Frames evidence for engineering review while preserving operational and compliance boundaries.
