Applications

Operational systems where relationships carry the signal.

Built first for interconnected water, pumping, and central-plant systems. Neraium can also evaluate other operational infrastructure when approved telemetry describes response across load, mode, schedule, or demand.

Representative evaluation contexts. Not claims of customer deployments.

Central plants and chilled-water systems

System context: Chillers, pumps, towers, valves, heat exchangers, distribution loops, and utility metering.

Representative relationships: Power to delivered flow, condenser-water response, staging sequence, pressure, flow, and temperature.

Review question: Did plant response change persistently under comparable load and operating mode?

Water and pumping systems

System context: Lift, transfer, distribution, industrial water, domestic water, and pumping infrastructure.

Representative relationships: Pump state, speed, flow, pressure, level, valve command, and delivered output.

Review question: Did delivered output weaken under comparable demand, and what can the telemetry not distinguish?

Campuses and large facilities

System context: Building automation, central utilities, and distribution systems serving multiple schedules, loads, and seasons.

Representative relationships: Air and water temperature response, valve command, differential pressure, and schedule context.

Review question: Which persistent change warrants engineering review before controls or maintenance work changes?

Resorts and commercial properties

System context: Central plants, water, pumps, valves, comfort infrastructure, and utilities without guest, payment, gaming, or reservation data.

Representative relationships: Load, flow, pressure, temperature, speed, staging, schedule, and weather context.

Review question: Does infrastructure behavior remain changed across comparable occupancy and operating modes?

Other telemetry-rich infrastructure

System context: Process-adjacent utilities and facility systems represented by approved historian, PLC-derived, database, API, or file-export telemetry.

Representative relationships: Utility demand, equipment command, temperature, pressure, flow, and approved operating context.

Review question: Is there enough comparable evidence to support a persistent behavioral finding without assigning root cause?
