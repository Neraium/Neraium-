Evidence Vault Protocol

Preserve the record. Examine the interpretation.

A finding is more useful when its evidence can be followed.

Evidence Vault Protocol (EVP) records Neraium-generated findings and their associated evidence. Where implemented, immudb-backed tamper-evident integrity helps verify that recorded information has not been altered.

Integrity protects the recorded information. It does not establish the correctness of an analytical conclusion. Scope and availability must be confirmed for each evaluation.

What EVP records

Neraium-generated findings and associated evidence. Where implemented, immudb backs tamper-evident integrity of those records.

What remains to validate

Data quality, model suitability, comparison context, and analytical conclusions still require scrutiny. EVP is not a certification, independent assurance of results, or guarantee of correctness.

Neraium is developing its MVP. EVP availability and implementation coverage must be confirmed for the agreed evaluation; no universal production deployment is implied.

A responsible outcome

Insufficient evidence is explicit.

If the history lacks comparable operating coverage, a responsible result may be insufficient evidence. That is a limit of the available analysis, not proof that the system stayed unchanged.
