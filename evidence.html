Representative evaluation structure. Not customer data or a customer case study.

EP-CHW-017

Pump power and delivered flow relationship weakened under comparable operating conditions.

Illustrative example. Not customer data.

Illustrative only
System
Chilled-water secondary pumping loop.
Behavioral Finding
Pump power increased relative to delivered flow and persisted across comparable operating periods.
Threshold Status
No limit was required to be exceeded.
Earliest Supported
First sustained during the overnight comparison window.
Operating Context
Comparable load band, same operating mode, similar commanded speed range, and similar distribution pressure target.
Supporting Evidence
Power-flow residual shift, pressure-flow response change, and repeated behavior across illustrative operating windows.
Confidence notes
Persistence: strong. Context match: moderate. Instrument agreement: partial. Explanation certainty: not claimed.
Evidence Limitations
Telemetry alone cannot distinguish hydraulic restriction from pump-performance degradation.
Recommended Starting Point
Verify valve position, inspect strainer differential pressure, and confirm commanded speed matches actual speed.

Engineering view

Shows relationship shifts, Operating Context, confidence dimensions, and evidence limits so engineering teams can decide what additional checks are justified.

Maintenance view

Summarizes what changed, what to check first, and what telemetry cannot determine yet in language usable for field review.

What it does not claim

The package does not claim root cause certainty, equipment failure, autonomous diagnosis, savings, downtime avoidance, or control-system authority.

Broader illustrative examples

These examples show relationship evidence beyond pump analytics. They do not invent failures or diagnoses.

Cooling tower staging

Finding: Cooling tower staging no longer produced the expected condenser-water temperature response under comparable heat-rejection load.

Limitation: Available telemetry does not establish whether the change came from airflow, water distribution, staging logic, or instrumentation.

Valve response

Finding: Valve command no longer produced the expected downstream temperature response under comparable load.

Limitation: Telemetry does not establish whether the difference came from valve position, flow availability, actuator behavior, or sensor error.

Sensor agreement

Finding: Redundant measurements began to disagree persistently under otherwise comparable conditions.

Limitation: The evidence identifies disagreement but does not establish which instrument is inaccurate.
