The compact artifact keeps the review question readable while preserving the evidence needed to understand its limits. Not customer data. Not a customer case study. Not a root-cause diagnosis.

EP-CHW-017 / CHILLED-WATER SECONDARY PUMPING LOOP

Power-to-flow behavior remained changed across comparable operating periods.

Illustrative
What changed
Pump power increased relative to delivered flow across repeated comparable operating periods.
Compared against what
A qualified reference under similar load, operating mode, commanded speed range, and distribution pressure target.
What supports it
Power-to-flow and differential-pressure-to-flow relationships, supported by pump, flow, pressure, speed, and load signals.
What remains uncertain
The evidence supports changed behavior, not a diagnostic probability or singular mechanical explanation.
What telemetry cannot prove
Telemetry alone cannot distinguish hydraulic restriction from pump-performance degradation.
What happens next
Engineering and operations validate context and decide whether continued analysis, field checks, or no action is appropriate.

Valid evidence outcome

Insufficient evidence is explicit, not hidden.

When the available data cannot support a responsible conclusion, the result says so.

OUTCOME / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
Outcome
No meaningful persistent change is supported by the available telemetry.
Why
Comparable operating coverage is incomplete.
Meaning
Absence of support is not proof that the physical system did not change.
Next step
Accept the outcome, refine the question, or identify additional approved context.
