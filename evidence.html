Findings and evidence

What changed, what supports it, and what remains unknown.

Neraium records what changed and the evidence that supports it. A persistent relationship change can be supported without claiming that a component failed or a root cause has been proven.

Observation

A bounded statement supported by telemetry.

Explanation

A cause remains unproven until people validate it in system context.

Evidence boundary

Support, uncertainty, provenance, and limitations remain attached.

Authority

What the finding means

A persistent behavioral departure is supported.

The observed system response no longer matches the established relationship under comparable conditions.

What it does not mean

A root cause has not been proven.

It does not prove which component failed or why. Telemetry alone cannot establish urgency, savings, or the correct operational action.

What happens next

People review a bounded technical question.

Engineering, operations, and maintenance teams validate context and decide whether further checks, continued analysis, or no action is appropriate.

Valid evidence outcome

Insufficient evidence is explicit, not hidden.

If signal quality, operating context, comparable history, or persistence does not support a responsible conclusion, Neraium can return “Insufficient evidence of meaningful persistent change.”

OUTCOME / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

No meaningful persistent change is supported by the available telemetry.

Reason
Comparable operating coverage is incomplete.
Limitation
The absence of support is not proof that the physical system did not change.
Next review
Engineering may accept the outcome, refine the question, or identify additional approved context.
