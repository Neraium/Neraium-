Review the Pilot

Purpose

Evidence when system behavior changes.

Neraium learns how operational signals normally behave together and presents persistent divergence for engineering review.

What it sees

Relationship change

Signals can remain in range while pressure, flow, temperature, demand, commands, and equipment response stop moving together as expected.

What engineers receive

Reviewable evidence

What changed, when it began, which relationships support it, whether it persisted, and what happened after an intervention.

Operating boundary

Read-only by design

Neraium does not issue commands, change setpoints, control equipment, diagnose root cause, or replace engineering judgment.

Evaluation

One real system

Use an approved historical baseline and comparison period, review findings with engineers, then decide whether live monitoring is justified.
