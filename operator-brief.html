Operator brief

What a Neraium finding means, and what it does not.

A finding is contextualized evidence for investigation. Your team decides what it means for the physical system.

Engineering review

Follow the evidence before deciding the action.

01

What changed?

Inspect the relationship, historical reference, source windows, and comparable operating conditions.

02

What supports it?

Review data coverage, signal quality, persistence, method identity, context, and limitations.

03

What remains uncertain?

Behavioral change does not independently establish root cause, degradation, or failure timing. Decide whether more context or a field check is warranted.

Two valid outcomes

Supported change, or insufficient evidence.

Not every dataset yields a finding. Insufficient comparable history can prevent a defensible conclusion, even when a real physical change exists.
