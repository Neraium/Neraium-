What a Neraium finding means, and what it does not.

A practical guide for teams reviewing evidence of persistent system change.

  • Read-only
  • Outside the control path
  • Not an alarm replacement
  • Human judgment authoritative
01

What does Neraium watch?

Approved telemetry describing signals, equipment state, relationships, and context within one bounded system.

02

What does a finding mean?

Telemetry supports a persistent departure from the established reference under comparable conditions.

03

What does it not mean?

It does not prove root cause, failure, urgency, savings, or the correct action. It is not an alarm or control command.

04

What should an operator do with it?

Validate context and instrumentation, apply site knowledge, and use existing engineering and maintenance procedures.

05

What remains under human judgment?

Interpretation, priority, safety review, field investigation, maintenance, control changes, and every operational action.
