Operator brief

Printable engineering and maintenance overview.

Neraium reviews supported telemetry for persistent behavioral changes and packages evidence so teams can decide where to investigate next.

What it does

Compares baseline and later behavior, evaluates relationships, and produces Evidence Packages when support is adequate.

What it does not do

It does not control equipment, replace engineering judgment, guarantee savings, or assert root cause certainty.

What technicians receive

A simpler view of what changed, what to check first, and what telemetry cannot determine yet.

How to start

Define one system, provide an approved export, review findings, and decide whether live read-only evaluation has value.

Maintenance view

Less complexity, not less honesty.

What we see

Pump power increased relative to delivered flow and remained changed across comparable operating periods.

What to check first

Verify valve position, inspect strainer differential pressure, and confirm commanded speed matches actual speed.

What we do not know yet

Telemetry cannot distinguish hydraulic restriction from pump-performance degradation.
