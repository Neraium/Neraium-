Operator brief

What a Neraium finding means, and what it does not.

A concise guide for operators and engineering leaders reviewing evidence of persistent change in interconnected infrastructure.

  • Read-only
  • Outside the control path
  • Not an alarm replacement
  • Human judgment authoritative
01

What does Neraium watch?

Approved operational telemetry that describes a bounded system: signals such as power, flow, pressure, temperature, command, load, equipment state, and relevant operating context.

02

What does a finding mean?

The available telemetry supports the conclusion that system behavior has persistently departed from an established baseline under comparable conditions.

03

What does it not mean?

It does not prove root cause, equipment failure, urgency, savings, or the correct action. It is not an autonomous diagnosis, alarm, or control command.

04

What should an operator do with it?

Validate the operating context and instrumentation, compare the evidence with site knowledge, and decide through existing engineering and maintenance procedures whether any checks are justified.

05

What remains under human judgment?

Interpretation, priority, safety review, field investigation, maintenance decisions, control changes, and every operational action.

Illustrative operator view

Evidence without unsupported certainty.

Not customer data. Not a root-cause diagnosis.

EP-CHW-017 / ILLUSTRATIVEChilled-water secondary pumping loop
What changed

Pump power increased relative to delivered flow and remained changed across comparable operating periods.

Threshold status

No individual signal limit needed to be exceeded.

What to validate

Valve position, instrument agreement, strainer differential pressure where available, and commanded versus actual speed under site procedures.

What remains unknown

Telemetry alone cannot distinguish hydraulic restriction from pump-performance degradation.

Possible outcome

Sometimes the responsible result is insufficient evidence.

If data quality, context, comparable coverage, or persistence is inadequate, Neraium should not force a finding. “Insufficient evidence of meaningful persistent change” is valid.

Starting point

Historical telemetry can come first.

A bounded evaluation can begin without a live connection, administrative credentials, or control-system changes.
