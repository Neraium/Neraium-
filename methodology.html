Methodology

How a finding is established.

Neraium compares mapped operating relationships against an approved baseline, checks context and data quality, requires persistence, and keeps the evidence available for engineering review.

Discuss an AssessmentReview the Method

Evaluation principles

Evidence must survive technical scrutiny.

01

Bounded comparison

The system, baseline, comparison period, included signals, and exclusions are defined before findings are reviewed.

02

Like-for-like context

Operating modes, demand ranges, staging, schedules, and known maintenance shape appropriate comparisons.

03

Visible support

Timing, affected relationships, persistence, and data-quality context stay attached to the finding.

Finding method

Six checks from source data to review.

The method is adapted to the selected system and approved data.

  1. 01

    Define the system boundary

    Identify the assets, signals, time period, operating question, and known exclusions.

  2. 02

    Map signals and context

    Confirm names, units, clocks, states, modes, staging, maintenance, and signal meaning.

  3. 03

    Establish normal relationships

    Build a baseline for how relevant signals behave together under represented conditions.

  4. 04

    Compare operating behavior

    Look for divergence from established relationships within technically appropriate context.

  5. 05

    Test persistence and data quality

    Separate transient noise and signal issues from behavior that remains across time or relevant cycles.

  6. 06

    Prepare evidence for review

    Present what changed, when it began, what supports it, and where investigation can start.

Finding states

Not every change becomes the same kind of output.

The output should make uncertainty and signal quality visible, not collapse them into one score.

Observed

Change under review

A relationship moved, but persistence or operating context is not yet sufficient.

Signal integrity

Data-quality issue

Stale values, gaps, timing shifts, flatlines, drift, or disagreement may affect interpretation.

Finding

Persistent divergence

The relationship change remains through the defined persistence and context checks.

Post-intervention

Change absent or remaining

The same relationship evidence is checked after repair, maintenance, or another intervention.

Engineering review

What the method cannot decide.

  • Whether a component has failed
  • The root cause of the observed behavior
  • Whether an intervention is required
  • The safety or operating consequence of a finding
  • Whether the available baseline represents every condition
  • What action the engineering team should take

Those decisions remain with qualified operators and engineers using site context.
