Methodology

Establish expected behavior. Then evaluate persistent change.

Neraium’s method is designed to answer a bounded question: does approved telemetry support the conclusion that an interconnected infrastructure system is behaving differently from its established operating reference?

  • Context preserved
  • Uncertainty explicit
  • Limitations retained
  • Human review authoritative

Evaluation sequence

A defensible finding starts before change detection.

  1. 01

    Approve and scope telemetry

    Define the physical system, review question, approved signals, time coverage, and customer-controlled transfer boundary.

  2. 02

    Evaluate signal usability and data quality

    Review timestamp integrity, continuity, coverage, measurement behavior, and whether the available signals can represent the question.

  3. 03

    Establish the operating baseline

    Identify historical behavior suitable for a reference. Historical does not automatically mean normal, and temporary abnormalities do not automatically redefine the baseline.

  4. 04

    Learn relationships and operating context

    Characterize expected response among signals while preserving load, mode, schedule, environment, and equipment state.

  5. 05

    Compare later behavior across time scales

    Evaluate later periods under comparable conditions and look for repeated, durable departures rather than isolated excursions.

  6. 06

    Construct the evidence boundary

    Record what is supported, which relationships and signals contribute, what is uncertain, where the data came from, and what telemetry cannot distinguish.

  7. 07

    Submit the outcome to human review

    Engineering, operations, and maintenance teams decide whether the evidence justifies validation, field checks, continued analysis, or no action.

Baseline learning

The operating reference is qualified before it is used.

Neraium does not ingest a file and immediately declare anomalies. A useful baseline must describe repeatable system behavior with enough signal quality and context to support comparison.

Timestamp quality

Ordering, gaps, duplicates, resolution, and comparable coverage.

Signal usability

Whether measurements are responsive, interpretable, and relevant to the system question.

Operating context

Whether load, mode, schedule, environment, and equipment state can be distinguished.

Baseline statistics

Expected ranges and variability without reducing the system to individual thresholds.

Expected relationships

Repeatable response across command, demand, energy, flow, pressure, temperature, and output.

Comparable operating context

Later behavior is judged against relevant conditions.

A relationship may change legitimately as operating conditions change. Neraium preserves approved context so comparisons remain technically meaningful.

Load

Plant or system demand.

Mode

Staging and operating configuration.

Schedule

Occupied, unoccupied, seasonal, or process timing.

Environment

Weather or other approved external conditions.

Equipment state

Command, availability, lead/lag, and known operating status.

Context improves comparison; it does not make incomplete telemetry certain.

Persistent and multiscale behavior

Duration alone is not enough.

A change must be considered across repeated operating periods and relevant time scales. Short disturbances, single transitions, and isolated points should not automatically become systemic findings.

Evidence construction

A finding is a bounded technical statement.

Telemetry can support an observation without proving why it occurred. Neraium keeps those two questions separate.

Valid outcomes

The method allows the evidence to say no.

Outcome A

Meaningful persistent change is supported.

The observation, context, relationships, uncertainty, provenance, and limitations are preserved for engineering review.

Outcome B

Insufficient evidence of meaningful persistent change.

Data quality, context, comparison coverage, or persistence does not support a responsible behavioral conclusion.

Human review remains authoritative. Neraium does not autonomously diagnose, assign root cause, change control logic, or direct equipment operation.
