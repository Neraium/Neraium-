Methodology

Establish expected behavior. Then evaluate persistent change.

Neraium’s method is designed to answer a bounded question: does approved telemetry support the conclusion that an interconnected infrastructure system is behaving differently from its established operating reference?

  • Context preserved
  • Uncertainty explicit
  • Limitations retained
  • Human review authoritative

Evaluation sequence

A defensible conclusion starts before change detection.

  1. 01Scope approved telemetry
  2. 02Check signal usability
  3. 03Establish the reference
  4. 04Learn relationships and context
  5. 05Compare across time scales
  6. 06Build the bounded finding
  7. 07Submit to human review

01 / Establish the reference

Qualify behavior before judging change.

Neraium reviews telemetry scope, timestamp and signal quality, suitable history, expected relationships, and whether the available context can support comparison. A reference is established only where the history is stable, relevant, and technically usable.

Known transient or abnormal operating periods do not automatically become the new normal.

  • Telemetry scope
  • Signal usability
  • Data quality
  • Baseline qualification
  • Reference protection
  • Expected relationships

02 / Compare like with like

Preserve the conditions around behavior.

A relationship can legitimately change as operating conditions change. Later behavior is compared under relevant context, not against history indiscriminately. Context improves the comparison without making incomplete telemetry certain.

  • Load
  • Mode
  • Schedule
  • Environment
  • Equipment state

03 / Look for persistent change

Duration alone is not enough.

Neraium looks across repeated operating periods and relevant time scales so short disturbances, isolated points, and normal transitions do not automatically become systemic findings. Support must remain meaningful under comparable conditions.

Short windowTransient response
Operating periodRepeated departure
Longer horizonPersistent behavior

04 / Build a bounded finding

Separate observation from explanation.

Telemetry can support the conclusion that behavior changed without proving why. A finding states what changed, the reference and relationships that support it, what remains uncertain, where the evidence came from, and what happens next.

What changed?

Evidence

Valid outcomes

The method allows the evidence to say no.

Outcome A

Meaningful persistent change is supported.

Evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and limitations remain attached.

Outcome B

Insufficient evidence of meaningful persistent change.

The available data cannot support a responsible conclusion.

Human review remains authoritative. Neraium does not diagnose root cause, change control logic, or direct equipment operation.
