Beyond individual thresholds

A system can change before any one signal crosses a limit.

BAS, SCADA, alarms, and operators remain essential. Neraium complements them by evaluating whether the relationships that describe system response have persistently changed.

Built first for central plants, pumping systems, water systems, and other interconnected facility infrastructure.

Traditional monitoring often asks
Did a signal cross a limit?
Neraium asks
Did the way the system behaves persistently change?

The result is a bounded review question: which relationship changed, under what conditions, and what deserves engineering attention?

How Neraium works

From approved history to a bounded engineering question.

Neraium qualifies the reference, compares like conditions, and preserves what the telemetry can responsibly support.

  1. 01

    Establish operating behavior

    Qualify approved history and establish a usable reference where the data supports one.

  2. 02

    Learn relationships and context

    Characterize how signals and systems respond across load, mode, schedule, environment, and equipment state.

  3. 03

    Identify persistent systemic change

    Compare later behavior across relevant time scales and comparable conditions.

  4. 04

    Preserve evidence for engineering review

    Surface the relationships that changed, with support and limitations intact.

EP-CHW-017

Power relative to delivered flow remained changed.

Illustrative

Not customer data. Not a root-cause diagnosis.

What changed
Pump power increased relative to delivered flow across comparable operating periods.
Supporting relationship
Power-to-flow behavior remained changed at similar load and operating mode.
Limitation
Telemetry alone cannot distinguish hydraulic restriction from pump-performance degradation.
