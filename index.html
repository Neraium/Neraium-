The monitoring gap

Individual readings tell
only part of the story.

Values can remain within expected ranges while the relationships between them change.

Conventional monitoring primarily evaluates individual values, alarms, and thresholds. Neraium evaluates how signals behave together over time, adding context for engineering investigation.

For example: comparable delivered flow may begin to require more pumping effort, even when both measurements remain within their individual limits.

How it works

From existing signals to engineering evidence.

  1. 01

    Connect existing data

    Use authorized, read-only access or an approved historical export. Agree scope and assess whether the data is suitable.

  2. 02

    Learn signal relationships

    Evaluate how effort, physical response, and operating context relate across a qualified historical reference.

  3. 03

    Identify persistent change

    Look for supported changes across comparable operating periods, including when individual signals stay within range.

  4. 04

    Review the evidence

    Present contextualized, traceable findings and limitations for engineering review. Insufficient evidence is a valid outcome.

Not every dataset supports a finding. Data suitability, comparable context, and limitations remain explicit.

Evidence Vault Protocol

Keep the evidence connected to the finding.

A finding is more useful when its evidence can be followed.

Evidence Vault Protocol (EVP) records Neraium-generated findings and their associated evidence. Where implemented, immudb-backed tamper-evident integrity helps verify that recorded information has not been altered.

Integrity protects the recorded information. It does not establish the correctness of an analytical conclusion. Scope and availability must be confirmed for each evaluation.
