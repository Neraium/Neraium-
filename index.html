Operational problem

Important changes can appear before a threshold alarm makes them obvious.

Facility systems are interconnected. A reading can remain inside limits while its relationship to load, pressure, temperature, command, or delivered output changes persistently.

Traditional monitoring asks

Did this measurement exceed a threshold?

Neraium asks

Has the system behavior persistently changed, and what evidence supports that conclusion?

What Neraium does

Relationship analysis organized for investigation.

Neraium evaluates approved historical exports or approved read-only sources. It focuses on relationships, persistence, operating context, explicit limitations, and human review.

Persistent behavior changes

Identify changes that remain across comparable operating periods.

Operating Context

Tie findings to load, mode, schedule, weather, staging, and known maintenance when those fields are available.

EP-CHW-017

Pump power and delivered flow relationship weakened under comparable operating conditions.

Illustrative example. Not customer data.

Illustrative only
System
Chilled-water secondary pumping loop.
Behavioral Finding
Pump power increased relative to delivered flow and persisted across comparable operating periods.
Earliest Supported
First sustained during the overnight comparison window.
Operating Context
Comparable load band, same operating mode, similar commanded speed range, and similar distribution pressure target.
Supporting Evidence
Power-flow residual shift, pressure-flow response change, and repeated behavior across illustrative operating windows.
Confidence notes
Persistence: strong. Context match: moderate. Instrument agreement: partial. Explanation certainty: not claimed.
Evidence Limitations
Telemetry cannot distinguish hydraulic restriction from pump-performance degradation.
Recommended Starting Point
Verify valve position, inspect strainer differential pressure, and confirm commanded speed matches actual speed.

Maintenance view

Less complexity, not less honesty.

What we see

Pump power increased relative to delivered flow and remained changed across comparable operating periods.

What to check first

Verify valve position, inspect strainer differential pressure, and confirm commanded speed matches actual speed.

What we do not know yet

Telemetry cannot distinguish hydraulic restriction from pump-performance degradation.
