How it works

From existing data to reviewable evidence.

  1. 01

    Connect existing data

    Historian exports, BAS, SCADA, CSV, databases, or approved read-only APIs.

  2. 02

    Learn operating relationships

    Build a baseline of how signals behave together under normal conditions.

  3. 03

    Detect persistent divergence

    Filter out short-lived noise and normal operating changes.

  4. 04

    Present evidence

    Show the affected relationships, timing, persistence, and investigation context.

Why engineers trust it

Built to support engineering judgment

Neraium acts as an extra set of eyes on chilled-water and hydronic behavior. The engineering team decides what the evidence means and what action, if any, to take.

  • Read-only by design
  • Never controls equipment
  • Findings require persistence
  • Data-quality issues are identified separately
  • Evidence remains visible
  • Engineers remain in control
