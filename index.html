Operational problem

Important changes can appear before a threshold alarm makes them obvious.

Facility systems are interconnected. A reading can remain inside limits while its relationship to load, pressure, temperature, command, or delivered output changes persistently.

Traditional monitoring asks

Did this measurement exceed a threshold?

Neraium asks

Has the system behavior persistently changed, and what evidence supports that conclusion?

What Neraium does

Three ways evidence becomes actionable.

Neraium turns approved operational telemetry into focused findings for engineering and maintenance review.

Persistent Behavioral Changes

Find changes that remain across comparable operating periods instead of reacting to isolated spikes.

The emphasis is on behavior that appears durable enough to investigate.

Operating Context

Frame each finding with available load, mode, schedule, weather, staging, and maintenance context.

Context helps teams separate signal from normal operational variation.

Evidence Packages

Organize the finding, support, limitations, confidence notes, and recommended starting points in one reviewable artifact.

Human engineering judgment remains authoritative.
