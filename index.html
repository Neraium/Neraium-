The monitoring gap

Everything can still be “in range” while the system has changed.

A threshold checks one signal against a limit. It cannot show whether the relationship among several in-range signals has changed.

Traditional monitoring
Did a signal cross a limit?
Neraium
Did the way the system behaves persistently change?

Neraium adds that relationship view without replacing alarms, controls, or operator judgment.

Operational consequences

A relationship change can matter before a threshold is crossed.

If it goes unseen, a team may encounter the change later as a delivery, resource, operator, or maintenance problem.

Degraded delivery

The same input may produce less useful output.

Rising resource demand

Comparable operation may require more power or runtime.

Operator burden

Teams may compensate for behavior alarms do not explain.

Maintenance ambiguity

Symptoms may appear without a clear place to begin.
