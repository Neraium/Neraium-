The monitoring gap

Acceptable readings.
Changing reality.

Infrastructure can deteriorate while conventional monitoring still shows acceptable operating conditions.

A pump can demand more energy to deliver the same flow. A cooling plant can work harder under a comparable load. The important change may lie between signals.
