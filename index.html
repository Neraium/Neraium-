See abnormal system behavior before it becomes obvious.

The Problem

Most teams find problems after the system is already acting up.

Alarms, inspections, and manual checks usually react to symptoms. Neraium watches how signals move together so teams can see when behavior starts changing earlier.

How It Works

A simple path from raw data to useful observations

What Neraium Helps With

Earlier visibility for systems that depend on stable conditions

Commercial pools and aquatic facilities

Watch chemistry, temperature, flow, equipment, and environmental changes together instead of one reading at a time.

Controlled environments

See when HVAC, humidity, irrigation, lighting, or sensor patterns begin drifting away from normal operation.

Cooling and infrastructure systems

Give operators a clearer early signal when connected systems stop behaving like their baseline.

Automation Support

Automated monitoring, not equipment control.

  • Does not control equipment or change setpoints.
  • Does not claim exact failure timing.
  • Does not replace operators, engineers, or maintenance teams.
  • Supports automated monitoring, alerts, and operator workflows.

Proof Point

Benchmark testing showed earlier signal visibility.

Dataset: NASA CMAPSS benchmark simulation, used for controlled testing.

Result: Neraium found persistent behavior changes while standard thresholds still looked normal.

Measured lead time: 198 cycles average, up to 520 cycles in the benchmark run.
