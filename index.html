The Problem

Most monitoring waits for a single number to cross a threshold.

Real operational problems often start earlier: flow and pressure drift apart, temperature and load stop moving together, or equipment behavior changes under the same conditions. Those changes can be missed when teams only watch individual readings.

What Neraium Does

It turns multi-signal telemetry into reviewable operating observations.

Reads existing data

Start with CSV history, telemetry exports, historians, or read-only system connections. No equipment control is required.

Compares relationships

Neraium looks at how readings move together, not just whether one value is high or low.

Shows what changed

The output is a plain observation: what behavior changed, where to look, and why it may deserve review.

Use Cases

Built for systems where stable relationships matter.

Commercial pools and aquatic facilities

Watch chemistry, temperature, flow, equipment, and environmental changes together instead of one reading at a time.

Controlled environments

See when HVAC, humidity, irrigation, lighting, or sensor patterns begin drifting away from normal operation.

Cooling and infrastructure systems

Give operators a clearer early signal when connected systems stop behaving like their baseline.

Deployment Boundaries

Monitoring support, not equipment control.

  • Read-only deployment: Neraium does not control equipment or change setpoints.
  • Operator review: findings are designed to support people, not replace them.
  • Scoped pilots: start with one system and evaluate usefulness before expansion.
  • Practical outputs: focus on what changed, why it matters, and where to inspect next.

Validation

Benchmark testing showed earlier signal visibility.

Neraium surfaced persistent behavior changes in NASA CMAPSS benchmark testing while standard thresholds still looked normal. Pilot work is used to verify whether those kinds of signals are useful in a customer's own environment.
