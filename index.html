Beyond individual thresholds

A system can change before any one signal crosses a limit.

BAS, SCADA, alarms, and operators remain essential. Neraium complements them by evaluating whether the relationships that describe system response have persistently changed.

Traditional monitoring often asks
Did a signal cross a limit?
Neraium asks
Did the way the system behaves persistently change?

Pump power, flow, and differential pressure may each remain inside their operating ranges while power required per unit of delivered flow changes materially.

How Neraium works

Establish the reference before judging later behavior.

Historical telemetry is not treated as an instant anomaly feed. Neraium first determines whether the signals and operating context can support a defensible comparison.

  1. 01

    Approved historical telemetry

    Begin with a bounded system and customer-approved data.

  2. 02

    Establish operating baseline

    Evaluate timestamps, usability, quality, and suitable reference periods.

  3. 03

    Learn signals, relationships, and context

    Preserve load, mode, schedule, environment, and equipment state.

  4. 04

    Compare later behavior

    Use comparable conditions and expected operating response.

  5. 05

    Identify persistent systemic change

    Evaluate repeated behavior across multiple time scales, not isolated noise.

  6. 06

    Preserve evidence and limitations

    Keep support, uncertainty, provenance, and what telemetry cannot prove together.

  7. 07

    Human engineering review

    People who understand the site decide what the evidence means and what happens next.

Baseline learning

Normal is established, not assumed.

Neraium establishes an operating reference before judging later behavior. Temporary abnormalities do not automatically redefine normal, and every dataset does not automatically produce a finding.
