How It Works

A simple path from telemetry to useful observations

01

Connect telemetry sources

Work from existing telemetry exports, CSV history, or read-only system connections.

02

Neraium learns normal behavior

The system compares current readings against normal relationships between signals.

03

Review what changed

Operators get a clear place to look, what changed, and how urgent it appears.

Use Cases

Earlier visibility for systems that depend on stable conditions

Commercial pools and aquatic facilities

Watch chemistry, temperature, flow, equipment, and environmental changes together instead of one reading at a time.

Controlled environments

See when HVAC, humidity, irrigation, lighting, or sensor patterns begin drifting away from normal operation.

Cooling and infrastructure systems

Automation Support

Automated monitoring, not equipment control.

  • Does not control equipment or change setpoints.
  • Does not claim exact failure timing.
  • Does not replace operators, engineers, or maintenance teams.
  • Supports automated monitoring, alerts, and operator workflows.
