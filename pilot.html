Evaluation

Historical Evaluation before live integration.

Start with one bounded system, approved historical telemetry, and a review question. Neraium first establishes data fit and comparison context; a live read-only connection is discussed only if the historical review justifies it.

Evaluation sequence

  1. Define one system and the engineering or operations question.
  2. Confirm which historical telemetry is approved and suitable for comparison.
  3. Coordinate a customer-approved transfer method.
  4. Establish baseline behavior and operating context.
  5. Compare a later period under comparable conditions where possible.
  6. Present supported findings with uncertainty and limitations, or explain why the evidence is insufficient.
  7. Decide whether continued historical analysis or live read-only evaluation has value.
