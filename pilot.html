Historical Evaluation before a live pilot.

Start with one bounded system, an approved historical export, and a review question to learn whether persistent behavioral change exists in the telemetry. A live pilot should follow only if the evidence and data fit justify it.

Evaluation sequence

  1. Define one system and review objective.
  2. Provide an approved historical export.
  3. Establish baseline behavior.
  4. Compare a later operating period.
  5. Determine whether a persistent behavioral change is supported by the evidence.
  6. Present supported findings with context, uncertainty, and limitations preserved.
  7. Decide whether a live pilot has value.
