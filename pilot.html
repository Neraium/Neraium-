Engagement process

A historical proof of value lets your team evaluate what existing operational data can support before considering live connectivity or commercial deployment.

The commercial path

Historical proof of value → Scoped paid pilot → Commercial deployment if validated.

01

Historical proof of value

Start with approved historical data. Assess suitability and what the available evidence can support before committing to deployment.

02

Scoped paid pilot

If the historical evaluation supports moving forward, agree a paid pilot with defined systems, interfaces, evidence criteria, and responsibilities.

03

Commercial deployment

Proceed if validated. Agree commercial terms, deployment boundaries, access, retention, and ongoing engineering review.

The first evaluation

One system. One practical question.

01

Agree the scope

Define the system boundary, engineering question, expected review outputs, and what would justify moving forward.

02

Qualify the data

Agree available signals, units, timing, operating context, data gaps, and an approved transfer method before exchanging telemetry.

03

Review the outcome

Assess traceable evidence and limitations, or an explicit insufficient-evidence result. A finding is not guaranteed.
