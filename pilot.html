Timeline

A practical 60-day pilot flow

Week 1-2

Data review and setup

Review available telemetry, map the target system, and prepare a read-only data flow.

Week 3-4

Baseline behavior mapping

Build a picture of normal operating behavior and look for readings that move together.

Week 5-6

Observation review

Review any abnormal-behavior observations with your operators and compare them to known events.

Week 7-8

Decision package

Deliver a clear summary of what worked, what did not, and whether expansion makes sense.
