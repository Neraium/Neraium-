How the blind evaluation works

  1. 01

    Choose one system and a known event

    Your team keeps the event details, exact timing, and known outcome private.

  2. 02

    Use separate baseline and evaluation datasets

    SII establishes a reference from the baseline dataset. The evaluation dataset includes the event without marking when it occurred.

  3. 03

    Evaluate and freeze the outcome

    SII compares the evaluation telemetry with the established reference and qualifies findings when the data supports them. Insufficient evidence is a valid result. The findings or insufficient-evidence result are frozen before comparison.

  4. 04

    Compare with what your team knows

    Review the frozen result against the event, its timing, and its known outcome. Your engineers decide what to investigate.
