What you receive

  • Assessment of whether the history supports your question
  • A usable operating reference, where supported
  • Traceable findings with context and limitations, or an explicit insufficient-evidence outcome

Engineering decides the next action. Findings are not guaranteed.

Evaluation process

  1. 01

    Define the review question

    Discuss the operating concern with your facilities or engineering team and define one bounded system to review.

  2. 02

    Scope available telemetry

    Agree signals, coverage, context, limitations, and transfer boundary.

  3. 03

    Establish a usable operating reference

    Qualify history where the data supports one.

  4. 04

    Evaluate persistent change

    Compare later behavior under relevant conditions and time scales.

  5. 05

    Review the outcome

    Discuss evidence and limitations with human judgment in control.

What your team provides

Discuss available signals and comparable operating periods. Scope and a customer-approved transfer method are agreed before telemetry is exchanged.

A qualified reference can support scoped Continued Analysis using later approved exports; live integration is not required.

Effort
Power, demand, runtime, or speed
Response
Flow, pressure, or temperature
Context
Load, mode, equipment state, and timestamps
Technical export requirements

Agree signal identity, units, timestamp alignment, sampling coverage, gaps, system boundaries, and relevant operating context. Data requirements depend on the question and available history.

Incomplete coverage or comparability can limit interpretation or yield insufficient evidence.
