Municipal water & wastewater

Municipal teams need reliable treatment and hydraulic delivery while managing energy demand and maintenance backlogs. Neraium examines how pumps, treatment processes, and connected equipment behave together, helping teams investigate developing change using existing telemetry.

Pumping stations & lift stations

Compare flow and pressure with pump speed, power, wet-well level, and demand across pumping stations and lift stations. Investigate sustained changes in effort relative to hydraulic delivery.

Treatment & connected processes

Review relationships across aeration, process measurements, connected equipment, and energy demand where signal coverage and context support them.

03

Distribution & collection

Examine interconnected pumping, pressure, flow, and level relationships across distribution and collection infrastructure. Demand, rainfall, seasonal conditions, and equipment state qualify the comparison.

Focus maintenance on supported change.

Utility managers can use supported changes in pumping effort and hydraulic response to prioritize investigation across competing maintenance needs. Plant operators and engineers can inspect the source history and operating conditions before deciding on a field check.

Maintenance teams can narrow the equipment and periods to review. Comparing energy demand with delivered flow helps distinguish changing demand from a performance concern and assess whether maintenance or additional operating expense is warranted.
