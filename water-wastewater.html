Illustrative review question

Has pumping effort changed relative to delivered flow under comparable conditions?

The question is specific. The evidence must be, too.

Compare relationships across appropriate history, account for load and mode, and retain the limits of what the data supports. A changing relationship can justify investigation without establishing why it changed.

These are potential evaluation scenarios, not validated customer outcomes or promised findings.
